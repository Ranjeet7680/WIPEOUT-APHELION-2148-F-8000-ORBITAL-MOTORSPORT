## 🏆 The 8 Race Event Archetypes

Every region provides 8 varied event modes to challenge different driving skillsets:

| Event Type | Focus & Rules | Scoring / Victory Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SPRINT** | Point-to-point high-velocity dash across 3–4 km. | Lowest elapsed time to the finish gate. |
| **CIRCUIT** | Multi-lap competitive race (2–3 laps) with 8 pilots. | First across the line after completing all laps. |
| **TIME TRIAL** | Solo run against the clock and ghost telemetry replay. | Beat the gold sector target time delta. |
| **DRIFT** | High-G magnetic slides through tight apex chicanes. | Accumulate drift points via slide duration, angle, and speed. |
| **ELIMINATION** | Survival mode where the last-place craft is detonated every 30s. | Be the final surviving pilot on track. |
| **CHECKPOINT** | Strict countdown clock extended by passing through blue gates. | Reach the final gate before time expires. |
| **NITRO CHALLENGE** | Endless boost zones and high-speed jumps. | Maintain over 380 km/h average speed. |
| **BOSS RACE** | 1v1 duel against the sector champion with adaptive AI. | Defeat the boss to claim the exclusive vehicle chassis. |

## 🌟 Star Rating System
- **1 Star ★**: Complete the race and cross the finish line safely.
- **2 Stars ★★**: Finish on the podium (Top 3) or beat the target time.
- **3 Stars ★★★**: Secure 1st Place with zero barrier collisions and at least 3 stunts.
