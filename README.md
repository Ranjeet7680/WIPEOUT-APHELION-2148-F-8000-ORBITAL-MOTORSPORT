## ⚡ Stunt & Arcade Racing Action Mechanics

- **Physical Stunt Ramps**: Located strategically across the 5.4 km circuit ($u = 0.22, 0.44, 0.82$).
- **Aerial Barrel Rolls**: Steer left/right during air time to perform a $360^\circ$ longitudinal roll (`BARREL ROLL +250 PTS`).
- **Aerial 360 Spins**: Engage boost in mid-air to trigger a full horizontal yaw spin (`360 SPIN +350 PTS`).
- **Perfect Landing**: Aligning the vehicle cleanly before touchdown grants `PERFECT LANDING +500 PTS`, $+35\%$ boost recharge, and combo multipliers (`x2 COMBO`, `x3 COMBO`).
- **Near Miss Bonus**: Weaving within $3.5\,\text{m}$ of autonomous civilian traffic awards `NEAR MISS +150 PTS` and boost energy.
- **Slipstream Drafting**: Tucking behind rival racers activates low-pressure slipstream (`SLIPSTREAM ACTIVE // +15% SPEED`).
- **3-Tier Hyper-Boost**: Normal Boost, Perfect Boost (timed sweet-spot release), and Overdrive Mode ($445\,\text{km/h}$ top speed with intense cyan/magenta plasma trails).

## 🏆 3D Victory Podium & Winning Progression

- **3-Tiered 3D Pedestals**: Gold (1st, Center), Silver (2nd, Left), Bronze (3rd, Right).
- **Physical Vehicle Mounts**: Top 3 finishers' vehicles are mounted onto the podium pedestals.
- **Victory Celebrations**: Winner vehicle revs engines, lifts front repulsor coils, and pulses neon lighting amidst confetti showers and spotlights.
- **Winning Headquarters**: Returns to the underground garage with the vehicle on the Champion Dais, tracking consecutive Win Streaks, XP Level progress, and unlocking rewards cards (Credits, Tokens, Rare Vehicle Cores).
